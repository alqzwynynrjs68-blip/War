# Kuwait map notes

The game map now includes the six governorates recognized by the State of Kuwait's administrative division:

- Al Asimah (العاصمة) — KW-KU
- Hawalli (حولي) — KW-HA
- Al Ahmadi (الأحمدي) — KW-AH
- Al Jahra (الجهراء) — KW-JA
- Al Farwaniya (الفروانية) — KW-FA
- Mubarak Al-Kabeer (مبارك الكبير) — KW-MU

The six regions are individually selectable in the SVG map. The capital city marker is rendered in Al Asimah.

Boundary geometry in this build is a simplified in-game approximation, not a cadastral or survey-grade official boundary dataset. The official six-governorate list and general administrative map are documented by the Kuwait Central Statistical Bureau in *Administrative Division of Kuwait* (2011): https://gis.csb.gov.kw/media/pdf/1_Chapter1_Administrative_Division_of_Kuwait.pdf
