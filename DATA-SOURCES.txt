WORLD DOMINION - GEOGRAPHIC DATA ATTRIBUTION

The regional physical geography vectors in js/region-data.js were extracted and simplified from the geographic boundary datasets bundled with Matplotlib Basemap:

- GSHHS coastline and lake polygons (intermediate resolution; GSHHS v2.3.6 dataset family).
- GMT / WDBII river and national-boundary line datasets.

Basemap documentation describes its GSHHS coastline data and its river/country boundary datasets:
https://matplotlib.org/basemap/stable/users/geography.html
https://matplotlib.org/basemap/stable/api/basemap_api.html

The basemap-data package identifies the bundled land-sea mask, coastline, lake, river, and political-boundary data as extracted from GSHHG and distributed under LGPL-3.0-or-later:
https://pypi.org/project/basemap-data/

GSHHG source and background:
https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/wessel/gshhg/

The map uses a regional subset and simplified vector paths for browser performance. Very short river segments are filtered; the geometry is geographic reference data, not a navigational or survey-grade dataset.

Global first-level administrative divisions (ADM1) were generated from the user-supplied geoBoundariesCGAZ_ADM1.geojson file. The supplied source contains 3,202 ADM1 features across 196 country/territory groups after excluding 19 disputed-area (DISP) features. Country outlines in js/map-data.js are dissolved from those ADM1 polygons for countries not previously present in the game. The original eight country outlines and their 94 ADM1 divisions from the uploaded game ZIP are retained unchanged in the final map and admin data files. Capital coordinates in js/capital-data.js were sourced from the CountryInfo country reference dataset, then aligned to the supplied ADM1 land polygons where the source point fell just outside the mapped region. The existing Iraq city set is retained. Small islands and multipart polygons are preserved where present in the supplied ADM1 geometry.
