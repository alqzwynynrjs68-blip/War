WORLD DOMINION — Geographic data attribution

Administrative boundaries: geoBoundaries, CGAZ ADM1 global dataset.
Source: https://www.geoboundaries.org/globalDownloads.html
The source dataset is distributed under the terms stated by geoBoundaries. Review the current license and attribution requirements before redistribution or commercial release.

This project contains simplified derivatives of the supplied ADM1 GeoJSON for browser performance. The supplied dataset contains 3,224 first-level administrative features. Country outlines are derived by dissolving ADM1 polygons by country code.
